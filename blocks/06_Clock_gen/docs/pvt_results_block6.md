Generated by `tools/summarize_block6.py` from the raw CSVs in `blocks/06_Clock_gen/results/pvt/`. All numbers: pre-layout ngspice, status `MEASURED_NOT_FINAL` / `SIMULATED_NOT_FINAL_PASS`; none is a final sign-off.

## Acceptance criteria used

These are engineering screening thresholds chosen for this design. They are **not** derived from a switch-settling or comparator-timing budget; the only system-level evidence is the nominal closed-loop run below.

| # | Criterion | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Phases are produced in every PVT condition (5 corners x -40/27/125 C x 1.62/1.8/1.98 V = 45) | 45/45 |
| A2 | No overlap: max over time of min(phi1, phi2) | < 1 % of VDD |
| A3 | Non-overlap gap (min of both gaps) | >= 5 ns |
| A4 | Phase width (min of both phases), T/2 = 125 ns | >= 100 ns |
| A5 | Comparator clock (phi2b through 2 inverters) rises after phi2 falls and before phi1 rises | 45/45 |
| A6 | Start-up: stable width and gap within a few clock cycles after the clock starts, VDD ramp 1 and 20 us | 4 corners |

## Comparator clock derived from phi2b (A5)

45 valid points; clock rises inside the non-overlap gap at 45/45. phi2 fall to clock rise: 0.28 .. 0.73 ns; clock rise to phi1 rise: 9.69 .. 19.68 ns. Load on the clock node is a placeholder (20 fF), not the extracted StrongARM input capacitance. Block 3 reports a worst-case integrated decision delay of 3.189 ns (standalone PVT campaign, 5 ns clock edges); the definitions and conditions of the two numbers have not been matched, so this is an indication, not a margin claim.

## Known limits (not covered)

- Pre-layout only: no extraction, no parasitics; clock-node load is a placeholder.
- No Monte Carlo (device mismatch, resistor spread). The SKY130 resistor model is not corner- or tempco-active in this ngspice setup; its model tempco (tc1 = -1.47e-3 /K, tc2 = 2.7e-6 /K) was applied manually to an ideal resistor, and R spread is covered only by x0.8 / x1.2 at the extreme conditions.
- Closed loop only at the nominal point (one amplitude, one bin, one run per configuration).
- During the supply ramp phi1 follows VDD until the clock starts and phi2 can rise to a fraction of VDD; the effect on the Block 5 switches is not evaluated. The first 2-3 clock cycles after start are not nominal; they are assumed to fall inside the 5 us reset, which is a default value, not a validated requirement.
- Supply current of the generator is estimated (about 2 x I) and not separately reported.
- Resistor length for 1.248 MOhm is about 850 um at W = 1.41 um (603 squares) with the SKY130 xhigh-po model; layout area is not evaluated.
